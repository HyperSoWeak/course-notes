# Computer Systems Lab Final Review Overview

## How to Study

The notes are organized by exam topic rather than by original source file:

1. `01_control_pid.md`: control, dynamics, PID, Lab04 stabilizer
2. `02_structures_trusses_springs.md`: statics, trusses, springs, catapult
3. `03_actuation_motors_transmission.md`: motors, friction, gears, pneumatics, hydraulics, micro actuation
4. `04_fabrication_cad_laser.md`: Fusion 360, 3D printing, FEA, laser cutting joints
5. `05_hci_history.md`: early HCI, Engelbart, Xerox PARC, Sketchpad, Memex
6. `06_final_project_boat.md`: maze-solving boat design notes

Recommended order:

1. Read `99_exam_quick_review.md`.
2. Study control/PID, structures, and fabrication.
3. Review actuation and HCI history.
